Who I Am

From operations floors to molecular dynamics.

I'm Ahmed Mohsin Ali, an MSc Bioinformatics student at Jamia Millia Islamia (2024–2026), working under Prof. Mansaf Alam on AI-guided molecular design, structural bioinformatics, and molecular dynamics for antimalarial drug discovery.

My path here wasn't linear. Before bioinformatics, I spent years managing healthcare operations across multiple countries — including as a Clinical Scientist at KAMC–MNGHA in Saudi Arabia — and I currently also serve as Branch Manager / Head of Operations at IBNSINA Travel & Tourism in Al-Hilla, Iraq. That operational background shapes how I work now: rigorous, process-driven, and comfortable managing many moving parts at once — whether that's a docking pipeline or a supply chain.

Today my research centers on Falcipain-2 inhibitor design, combining generative AI, structure-based drug design, and wet-lab validation. Alongside my own research, I review manuscripts for bioinformatics and computational chemistry journals, and contribute to book chapters on AI-guided therapeutic design.
